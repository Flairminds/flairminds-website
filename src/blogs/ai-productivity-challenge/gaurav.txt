AI Productivity
1. Meeting Intelligence & Auto-Documentation(fireflies.ai)
Problem: Meetings are time-consuming, notes are fragmented and action items get lost.
Solution: An AI tool that integrates with calendar/video conferencing apps like teams.
● Records & Transcribes meetings in real-time.
● Generates & Distributes concise summaries with clear decisions
● Store transcripts in a searchable knowledge repository, allowing users to quickly
search past meetings by keywords, topics, or decisions
2. Document Intelligence with Smart Q&A (NotebookLM)
Problem: We spend excessive time reading lengthy documents (requirements, research,
reports, specs) and struggle to find specific information buried within them.
Solution: An internal AI assistant with RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) capability
that allows teams to interact with documents conversationally.
● Generates visual mind maps to show document structure, key concepts, and
relationships
3. Smart Message & Notification Filter (Email / WhatsApp / Teams)
(n8n+LLM)
Problem: We receive a large number of emails and WhatsApp messages daily—most of
them are ads, promotions, or irrelevant updates. While ignoring these, important
messages (approvals, deadlines, escalations) often get missed.
Solution: Using n8n and AI, we can automatically filter emails and WhatsApp messages,
notify only what truly matters, and ignore the noise.
● Prevents missing critical information
● Reduces notification fatigue
4. AI-Driven Development (Cursor, Windsurf)
Problem: Software development involves repetitive and time-consuming tasks such as
writing boilerplate code, fixing bugs, creating test cases, and performing manual code
reviews—slowing down overall delivery.
Solution: Leverage AI-powered coding assistants integrated into IDEs and workflows
(e.g., Cursor, Windsurf, GitHub Copilot, Tabnine) to accelerate the entire development
lifecycle.
● Understands project context and coding standards for consistent output
Conclusion - AI is not meant to replace employees but to remove repetitive work, reduce
cognitive load, and improve decision-making. By integrating AI into daily workflows—meetings,
coding, documentation, and planning—we can significantly enhance productivity, quality, and
efficiency
