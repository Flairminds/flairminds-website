Dear Team,
To encourage practical and impactful use of AI in our daily work, we are launching an AI Productivity Challenge for all employees.
Objective
The goal of this initiative is to identify how AI can help us improve productivity, quality, and efficiency in our day-to-day work—whether in software development, project management, documentation, analysis, communication, or time management.
What You Need to Do
Each employee is required to submit a 1-page document describing:
How you currently use AI to improve your productivity OR
An innovative AI-based idea that can be implemented in your daily life. 
Both implemented use cases and new ideas are welcome.
Submission Guidelines
Format: 1-page maximum 
Submission Type: Individual
Deadline: Monday 5 Jan 2026. 
Evaluation & Recognition
The submissions will be reviewed to select top 3 ideas. Selected participants will have the opportunity of presenting their ideas to the whole team. The winner will
Receive exciting prize
Be recognized as AI Productivity Champions
Feature on company's website and post
Important Note
This challenge is not about advanced AI knowledge. Simple, practical use cases that save time or improve quality are highly encouraged.
Let’s use this opportunity to learn from each other and build a stronger, more efficient way of working.
Compulsory for all associates. Everyone
